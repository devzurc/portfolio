# Responsive specification

## Breakpoints

- Below 640px: 48px hero title, single-column content, compact gutters, stacked form fields, two-column statistics.
- 640–767px: mobile disclosure remains active; cards stay single-column.
- 768–1023px: desktop navigation appears; hero title is 72px; layouts collapse where content would become cramped.
- 1024px and above: 96px hero title and full desktop composition.

## Required captures

The QA matrix covers 1920×1080, 1440×900, 1280×800, 1024×768, 768×1024, 430×932, 390×844, and 375×812 at device scale factor 1.

Every viewport must have no horizontal overflow, clipped controls, or unexpected ordering. Touch targets are at least 44px. Portuguese copy may wrap more deeply but must preserve hierarchy and content parity.
