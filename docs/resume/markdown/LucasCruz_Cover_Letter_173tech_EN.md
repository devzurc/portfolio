# Lucas Cruz — Cover Letter

173tech · Senior Data Analyst · London

Dear Hiring Team,

I am applying for the Senior Data Analyst role at 173tech. I am a Senior Data Engineer and Gen. AI Automation Engineer with 5+ years of experience, and the work I want to be hired for is the part of my background that matches your engagements: taking an unclear operational problem, modelling the data, and delivering something a team can use. I am based in Florianópolis, Brazil. I do not currently have the right to work in the UK. I would relocate for this role under Skilled Worker visa sponsorship.

The closest match to your client work is my consulting at wDiscover. I delivered data projects across fintech, agribusiness, and operations, designed dimensional warehouses on SQL Server and Oracle, and built Power BI dashboards for financial, operational, and KPI tracking. Before that, as a Data Analyst at Itaeté Group, I designed a SQL Server warehouse and Python/SQL pipelines and cut reporting processing time by ~50% by replacing manual Excel/VBA workflows with Power BI and automation. At Spacecom Monitoramento I maintained an AWS lakehouse for 95,000+ IoT devices, reduced pipeline failures by ~30%, and raised downstream analytics accuracy by ~40% so Qlik Sense and Apache Superset had datasets people could trust.

In my current contract at TK Technologies I build Python pipelines and LLM/n8n workflows that turn messy pricing data into something business users can query, and I run customer demos, product training, and delivery sprints. That is the AI and stakeholder side of the role. My production data modelling is SQL, not a multi-year client dbt practice. I have used dbt in personal projects only: dbt Core on a local DuckDB lakehouse, and a Snowflake/Airflow lab. The BI tools I have shipped with are Power BI, Qlik Sense, and Apache Superset.

I would like to bring that mix of warehouse modelling, governed reporting, and practical AI workflows to 173tech.

Sincerely,

Lucas Cruz

Florianópolis, Santa Catarina, Brazil
