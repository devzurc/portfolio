## Roles to be careful with

- Pure prompt-engineering roles with little backend/data/platform ownership.
- Pure low-code/no-code roles where engineering depth is not valued.
- Pure sales or customer success roles without hands-on technical delivery.
- Data analyst-only roles that center mostly on dashboards and ad hoc reporting. Apply only when the job also asks for modelling, delivery ownership, or AI workflows and names a sponsorship path.
- Roles requiring local work authorization with no sponsorship path.
- Roles that need monthly on-site time in the UK, Ireland, the Netherlands, Portugal, or Spain unless you are willing to relocate on that country's work visa. Do not treat "remote-first" as remote from Brazil.

### Weekly filter

Target 5–8 sponsorship-qualified applications a week. For each one, log company, role, country, sponsor evidence, and status in `docs/career/goals/job-applications.md`. Keep the public CV generic. Tailor only the application copy.

Before spending time on a posting:

1. Sponsorship is explicit, or the legal entity is on the official register below.
2. The work matches evidence you can defend. For analyst roles, that is SQL modelling, Power BI / Qlik / Superset, data quality, and optional LLM workflows.
3. Location policy is compatible with relocating on that visa. A UK "remote-first, one day a month in London" role means living in the UK.
4. Salary still matters after sponsorship. For a UK Skilled Worker application checked on 22 Sep 2026, the standard floor is £41,700 and the data-analyst going rate (SOC 3544) is £34,900. Count guaranteed base pay, not a discretionary bonus.
