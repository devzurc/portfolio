# AI roadmap tracking

## Trigger

Use for roadmap progress reviews, capstone planning, or evidence-backed goal updates in `docs/career/goals/`.

## Inputs

- The relevant goal or monthly plan.
- Verified public evidence: repository links, curated project profiles, or CV text.
- The review period and desired outcome.

## Workflow

1. Read the applicable roadmap files and `docs/career/INDEX.md`.
2. Separate completed, in-progress, and proposed work; do not turn plans into achievements.
3. Map completed work to evidence and identify the smallest meaningful next milestone.
4. Draft changes and public-facing implications separately. Portfolio or CV changes require owner approval.

## Output

Return progress status, evidence links/paths, gaps, next actions, and any proposed updates. Preserve the existing roadmap structure.
